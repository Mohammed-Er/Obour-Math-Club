What we're here to do

OMC exists for two kinds of students: the ones chasing a competition medal, and the ones who just want a weekly reason to think about something interesting. We run olympiad training, host open problem-solving nights, and build the kind of small community where explaining your solution to someone else is half the fun. No one is turned away for being "not competitive enough" — the club is built to widen, not gatekeep.
